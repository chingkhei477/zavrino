Reading an offer

What each offer card tells you

Read the card before you click — the conditions on it are the ones that decide your cashback.

Reward

The cashback agreed with the store — a percentage of the eligible order value or a flat amount. “Up to” means the top rate applies only to some products.

Tracks

How long the store usually takes to report your order to us. Send your claim after you order; we'll match it once it appears.

Confirms in

How long the store takes to approve the sale, usually after its return or cancellation window closes.

Reward conditions

Excluded products, payment methods, coupon rules, app vs website and any first-order-only limits.

Valid till

The last date the offer applies. Expired offers are removed from this page automatically.

Before you shop: turn off ad-blockers for the store, finish the purchase in the same browser tab, and don't visit another coupon or cashback site in between. These steps help the store attribute your order to Zavrino. Cashback is paid only on orders the store confirms — see the Cashback Policy.
