About Zavrino

Cashback, explained honestly

Zavrino is an Indian cashback and rewards website based in Bishnupur, Manipur. We bring you partner store offers and share the commission we earn with you — with the rules written out in plain language.

What we do

When you shop at a partner store through a Zavrino link, the store pays us a commission for the referral. We pass part of that commission back to you as cashback.

Cashback often disappoints because the conditions are hard to find. So every Zavrino offer shows its reward, tracking time, confirmation time and exclusions on the offer itself, and our policies explain what happens at each step — including when cashback is declined.

What we don't do

  • Publish reviews, ratings or statistics we can't back up.
  • Promise or guarantee earnings.
  • List offers before a partnership is live.
  • Charge you to use Zavrino or to withdraw cashback.
  • Ask for OTPs, UPI PINs, card numbers or passwords.
  • Run advertising or tracking scripts on this website.
Our principles

How we work

Real offers only

A store appears on Zavrino only when the partnership is active and tracking is tested.

Conditions up front

Reward rules sit on the offer card, not behind a link.

Plain-language policies

Short, readable policies that say what we do and what we don't.

Privacy first

We collect only what's needed to verify claims and pay you.
