Eligibility

What makes a purchase eligible

Stores pay cashback only when they can attribute the sale to Zavrino and the order is final.

  • You opened the store using the Shop via Zavrino button on a live offer.
  • You completed the purchase in the same browser session, without switching to another coupon or cashback site.
  • The products and payment method are not excluded in the offer's conditions.
  • You sent your claim within 30 days of the order date.
  • The order was not cancelled or returned, and the store confirmed it.
Worth knowing

What usually stops cashback

  • Ad-blockers or privacy settings that block the store's tracking cookies.
  • Applying a coupon code that isn't listed on the Zavrino offer.
  • Visiting another cashback, coupon or price-comparison site before paying.
  • Buying through the store's app when the offer says website only.
  • Cancelling, returning or exchanging the order.

Transparency

How Zavrino is paid — and what we never do

Partner stores pay Zavrino a commission when an eligible purchase comes through our link. We share part of that commission with you as cashback. That is our whole business model.

No fees to you

Browsing offers, sending claims and withdrawing cashback are free. Your bank's own charges, if any, are set by your bank.

We never ask for OTPs

Zavrino will never ask for an OTP, UPI PIN, card number, CVV or password — by email, phone or message.

No promised earnings

Cashback depends on the store confirming your order. We don't guarantee amounts and we don't publish made-up earnings.
